# 0021: Payments, entitlements and the ledger

**Status:** accepted (B5.1, 22 November 2026; gateways and routing B5.2a; payouts B5.2b; ledger, credits, pools, points, refunds and reconciliation B5.3).

## Context

REOS sells plans, courses, packages, credit packs and seat packs to candidates, agents, brokerages, schools, providers and associations, with a price per state (decision 3), sponsors paying part, and Texas sales tax. Buying happens on the web only (screen 3); the phone apps never sell (decision 4). Course-commerce tools tie access to one gateway's subscriptions; Stripe's own Entitlements cover subscriptions but not one-time purchases. Webhooks arrive late or twice.

## Decision

1. **Catalogue (screen 67).** `products` (kind, buyer, what's included, the features they unlock as entitlement keys such as `learn.qualifying`, AI help) and `prices` per state and interval (once, month, year) in USD with an optional floor. Prices are never edited: a change ends the current price and starts a new one, so the history stays and subscribers keep their price until renewal.
2. **Sponsor discounts** (`sponsor_discounts`): a brokerage, association or school pays part of a product for its people; checkout applies the largest one automatically, never below the price's floor.
3. **Checkout (screen 3).** An order is created with the quote, then the gateway's own payment fields take the card: Stripe embedded Checkout (PCI SAQ A; REOS never sees card data) with Stripe Tax (`automatic_tax`, address collected, prices tax-exclusive), one customer per person and environment, and idempotency keys per order. The Simulator (outside production) shows a clearly marked test panel. Off pauses purchases.
4. **Unlock at once (addition 5).** On return REOS asks the gateway for the session (`status: complete`, `payment_status: paid`) and fulfils the order in a locked transaction: paid, totals and tax, receipt link, entitlements, a subscription row for plans, one receipt email. The webhook and `reos:billing:reconcile` (every 10 minutes) call the same code, so a missed or repeated event can't skip or double anything.
5. **REOS keeps its own entitlements** (`entitlements`: person, feature, source, state, starts, ends, revoked), fed by orders (no end), subscriptions (period end plus two days, so a slightly late renewal doesn't close access), sponsors and admins. The web and `GET /api/v1/me/entitlements` read them; the app asks on start and on returning to the front, so a purchase unlocks the phone within seconds. Course access is `learn.<course type>` in the person's state; each course's first lesson is free.
6. **Webhooks** (`/webhooks/stripe`): `Stripe-Signature` checked (five-minute tolerance), each event stored once by id (`webhook_receipts`), handled on the `billing` queue: checkout completed, invoice paid (renewal), payment failed (past due, a 7-day grace period, an email), subscription deleted (access ends), charge refunded in full (access revoked).
7. **Plan and billing** (screen 29 and each role's equivalent, in Settings): plans for the person's role and state, what they have now, subscriptions (cards and cancelling in Stripe's customer portal) and receipts.
8. **The phone never sells (B5.1b, decision 4).** The Membership screen (candidate screen 6) shows what a plan includes, never a price. iPhone: "Email me the link" by default; a super-admin switch on screen 67 (`platform_settings`, audited) turns on the reos.com link without an app release. Android shows the same words without a link. `GET /me/membership`, `POST /me/membership/email-link`.
9. **Sponsor codes** (`sponsor_codes`, `sponsor_code_redemptions`): REOS issues a code for seats a tenant pays for on the tenant's Plan tab (screen 60); each person redeems once ("Enter a code" in the app or on Plan and billing) and gets the product's features as a `sponsor` entitlement; codes stop at their seats, expiry or when stopped. B5.3 funds them from sponsor pools.
10. **More gateways, one interface (B5.2a).** `PaymentGateway` (checkout, result, portal, signed events) has Stripe, PayPal (Orders v2 with PayPal's hosted approval, captured on return; webhooks checked with PayPal's verify-webhook-signature) and Authorize.net (Accept Hosted: REOS gets a token and posts the buyer to Authorize.net's page; the result is found by invoice number; webhooks signed with HMAC-SHA512). Each says whether it takes plans that renew (Stripe only) and whether it works out tax (Stripe only). Card data never touches REOS at any of them (PCI SAQ A). Webhooks: `/webhooks/{stripe|paypal|authorize-net}`; receipts are kept per gateway, and events find orders by gateway and session.
11. **Routing and the kill switch (screen 66).** `gateway_settings` says which states and kinds of product each gateway serves (none: all), which is the default, and whether tenants may connect their own account (gateways with payouts only). Checkout tries the default first, then the next in order, skipping paused gateways, gateways that are Off or lack keys, and gateways that can't take a plan that renews; none left means "Purchases are paused". Keys and Off / Simulator / Sandbox / Live stay in Settings › Integrations ("Add gateway" goes there). Every change, pause (with its reason) and resume is audited with before and after.
12. **The same tax everywhere (B5.2a).** Gateways without built-in tax ask for the buyer's ZIP code first; Stripe's tax calculation API works out the tax from state and ZIP code, the gateway charges that amount, and after payment the calculation is recorded as a Stripe Tax transaction for filing. Without Stripe keys outside production, the Simulator's demo rate (8.25%) stands in; in production checkout refuses rather than charge no tax.
13. **Payouts (B5.2b).** A product may be sold by a school (`products.seller_organisation_id`, set on screen 67); its sales are split when the order is paid (`payout_lines`, once per order and payee): REOS's platform fee first (screen 66, 10%, covering card fees; tax is never shared), then each instructor's or mentor's percent of the rest (`product_splits`, "Revenue split" on screen 67, at most 100%), and the school keeps what's left. Products REOS sells earn no one else anything.
14. **Stripe Connect, separate charges and transfers.** Schools, instructors and mentors set up payouts on Stripe's own Express onboarding (bank and tax details never touch REOS); a school may connect its own Stripe account instead (Connect OAuth) when screen 66 allows tenant accounts for Stripe. Every Monday `reos:payouts:run` sends each ready payee one transfer for all pending lines (idempotency key = payout id; lines are claimed first so two runs can't pay twice); payees still setting up keep earning. Because transfers draw on the platform balance, PayPal and Authorize.net sales are paid out the same way. A refund cancels unpaid lines and reverses paid ones (transfer reversal). `account.updated` webhooks (`/webhooks/stripe-connect`, own signing secret) keep account status current; the run also re-checks accounts not yet ready.
15. **Statements.** School: Sales and payouts (screen 53: products with prices, sold this month and sponsor discounts; gross, sponsor discounts, platform fee, instructor and mentor payouts, net; where payouts go; recent payouts; CSV export). Instructors and mentors: Earnings on the web and in the app (screens 20 and 28, `GET /me/earnings`). Screen 66 shows what's waiting, sent this month and accounts ready.
16. **The ledger (B5.3).** `ledger_accounts` (people's monthly and top-up credits, points and earnings; organisations' credit and seat pools and earnings; platform revenue, sales tax, sponsor discounts, refunds, AI pool and use, credits sold, seats and points issued; one clearing account per gateway and environment) and `ledger_entries` (transaction id, amount, unit `usd` cents, `credit`, `seat` or `point`, balance after, reason, reference). Every transaction balances to zero per unit; entries are never edited or deleted (the model refuses); balances are updated under row locks taken in id order. Business events post once (checked by reason and reference): order paid (clearing, tax, the sponsor discount as its own entry, revenue, each payee's earnings), payout, payout reversed, full refund (the order's entries reversed exactly), partial refund (from revenue).
17. **AI credits** (`AiCredits` now reads the ledger). Each person's monthly credits (the largest of their active plans and sponsored seats, else `reos.ai.monthly_credits`) arrive the first time they're needed each month, and last month's leftovers expire, once per person and month. Use takes monthly credits first, then top-ups (credit packs, never expire). Candidates are never charged: their use draws on a sponsor's credit pool, else the platform's. Usage is still logged by feature and engine (never content).
18. **Sponsor pools and points.** REOS adds credits and seats a tenant paid for on its Plan tab (screen 60, with the invoice or contract as a note, audited); new sponsor codes draw seats from the pool and a stopped code returns its unused seats (tenants without a funded pool keep B5.1b's behaviour). Points accounts take awards once per milestone and redemptions never beyond the balance; earning rules come in Sprint 13.
19. **Refunds from REOS** (screen 66, Orders and refunds; `ConfirmIdentity`). Full or partial, through the order's gateway with an idempotency key per refund (Stripe refunds, PayPal capture refunds, Authorize.net void or refund). A full refund ends access, cancels or reverses shares and reverses the ledger; a partial one keeps access and comes from revenue. The policy check (full refund within 7 days; course progress under 25% once the player tracks it) is shown and stored; refunds outside it need a reason and are marked.
20. **Daily reconciliation** (`reos:ledger:reconcile`, 05:20): yesterday's orders, refunds and payouts matched amount for amount with Stripe's balance transactions (by payment intent, refund and transfer id), or, for PayPal, Authorize.net and the Simulator, with what each order's gateway confirmed; plus a check that every balance equals its entries. Runs and differences show on screen 66.
21. **Records.** Orders stay for tax records when an account is deleted, linked to no one. Price changes, plan changes and orders are audited.

## Consequences

- Workers listen on the `billing` queue; the Stripe webhook endpoint subscribes to `checkout.session.completed`, `checkout.session.async_payment_succeeded`, `invoice.paid`, `invoice.payment_failed`, `customer.subscription.deleted` and `charge.refunded`.
- Stripe Tax needs the Texas registration in the client's Stripe account before live mode.
- PayPal webhooks subscribe to `CHECKOUT.ORDER.APPROVED`, `PAYMENT.CAPTURE.COMPLETED` and `PAYMENT.CAPTURE.REFUNDED`; Authorize.net to `net.authorize.payment.authcapture.created`, `.refund.created` and `.void.created`.
- Stripe stays required for tax even when another gateway takes the payment.
- Stripe Connect needs the platform profile approved and a Connect webhook endpoint (`account.updated`) before live payouts; renewals of school plans and direct charges on a tenant's own account are not split yet (school products are one-time today).
- Sprint 6 builds screen 69 (AI costs and limits) on these credits; Sprint 8's player supplies course progress for the refund policy; Sprint 13 sets the points rules.
