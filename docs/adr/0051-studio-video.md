# ADR 0051: Studio video

- Status: Accepted (listing videos and the editor, B12.2)
- Date: 2026-11-24 (Sprint 12; spec in docs/sprints/S12-spec.md)

## Context

Short vertical video gets the most reach on Instagram Reels, TikTok and YouTube Shorts. B11.3 can post a video. Agents still need a way to make one from a listing without learning an editor. Lofty, Rechat and Canva make listing videos, but leave the broker's name, fair housing wording and the brokerage's policy to the agent. Screen 168 asks for simple editing: trim, captions, music, brand and hook. It also asks that pauses be taken out automatically, that only royalty-free music be used, and that the brand frame come from the brand kit.

## Decision

1. **A video is a list of scenes, kept and editable.**
   - `studio_videos` stores the hook (the first two seconds' words) and the scenes. Each scene has a photo or clip, a start, a length and a caption.
   - It also stores the music, whether the brand colour is on, and the rendered file's version.
   - A listing video takes up to eight of the photos the MLS lets the agent post, any of the agent's own clips and photos after the first photo, and a closing card in the brand colour with the call ("Call James Miller to see it").
   - Photo lengths are stretched so the video runs about 30 seconds. The whole video is capped at 60 seconds and 12 scenes (the Shorts limit).
2. **Words are checked like posts.**
   - The hook and the captions go through `AdvertisingPolicy`: fair housing wording, claims and the brokerage's banned phrases.
   - Words are cleaned when made. After every edit they are checked again and the result is shown.
   - A video with flagged words is not rendered until Fix automatically or an edit clears them.
   - Hook suggestions come from Claude when configured, otherwise from templates. They are free.
3. **ffmpeg in the queue worker, behind `VideoRenderer`.**
   - `FfmpegRenderer` renders each scene to a uniform segment (1080 × 1920, 30 fps, H.264 and AAC). Stills drift slowly in or out (`zoompan`). Clips are trimmed and cropped to fill the frame.
   - Segments are joined, then one pass adds the frame, the hook, fades and the music. It uses the veryfast preset; a 30-second video takes well under a minute.
   - `SimulatedRenderer` (CI, or `REOS_VIDEO_RENDERER=simulator`) copies a one-second placeholder and draws a real poster. Everything else can then be tested without ffmpeg.
   - A Pest test renders a real 30-second MP4 wherever ffmpeg is installed, and is skipped elsewhere.
   - ffmpeg is in the preview and dev container images.
4. **Words drawn with GD, not ffmpeg's drawtext.**
   - Captions, the hook and the frame are transparent PNG overlays drawn with Inter by `VideoFrames`.
   - Text then looks the same with every renderer. There is no filter escaping of user text, and no font setup on the worker.
   - The frame puts the agent and the broker on one line at one size (22 TAC §535.155). Turning the brand colour off gives a plain dark band; the names always show.
5. **Pauses are taken out; filler words come later.**
   - For clips with sound, `silencedetect` (−35 dB, 0.7 s) finds pauses. Only speech is kept, with 0.15 s of air either side, using `select` and `aselect`.
   - Removing filler words needs a transcript. It joins when speech-to-text is wired for clips.
6. **REOS-made music.**
   - Three tracks (`calm`, `bright`, `none`) are generated by ffmpeg's `aevalsrc` from chord expressions.
   - They are written, not recorded, so REOS owns them and no licence can lapse.
   - They play under the clips' own sound at half volume, fading at the end.
   - A licensed library can replace them later under the same names.
7. **Credits.**
   - Making a video costs `short_video`: 30 credits, shown first.
   - Rendering again after edits is free. Screen 166's rule: regenerating uses credits only for videos, and an edit is not regenerating.
8. **Files.**
   - The video and poster are stored as `social/<agent>/studio/<uuid>-v<n>.mp4` and `.jpg` on the content disk. A new render gets a new name, so phones never play a stale copy, and the old files are deleted.
   - The signed media route now answers byte ranges on the local disk and redirects to a short-lived S3 link otherwise, as phones need to play video.
   - Posting the video uses B11's engine unchanged.

## Consequences

- The queue worker needs ffmpeg (6.x locally; Debian bookworm's 5.1 in the image) and `/tmp` space for one render at a time per worker. `RenderStudioVideo` is unique per video, allows 900 s and makes two tries; a failure says so on the video and keeps the last good render.
- Web screen 17 makes listing videos, plays them and uses them in a post. Editing is on the phone (screen 168), as the journeys place it.
- Not yet: filler-word removal, a licensed music library, and B-roll from the agent's camera roll on the web.
