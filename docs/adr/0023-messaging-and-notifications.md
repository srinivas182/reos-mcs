# 0023: Messaging and notifications

**Status:** accepted for messaging (B6.1a and B6.1b), the notification centre (B6.2a), push (B6.2b) and texts (B6.2c), 22 November 2026; email bounces (B6.2d) extend it.

## Context

Brokerages, schools and mentors talk to their people in Slack, texts and CRM inboxes today, mixing clients, colleagues and vendors with no rules. REOS needs messaging that only connects people who work together, arrives live on the web and the phone, and keeps tenant data separate. People hold roles in several organisations, and a team (its own tenant) sits under a brokerage (another tenant).

## Decision

1. **Relationships decide who can message whom** (`App\Messaging\Relationships`), never a free directory. Within an organisation, its parent and its teams: broker with agents, mentors and other brokers; agents with each other only inside their team; school admins and instructors with their students and each other; mentors with their mentees (agents or students) and school staff; association admins with each other. Providers join when an agent accepts their request (provider app). REOS support works as the tenant's admin only inside an approved support session (A4.1c). A one-to-one also needs the relationship to still hold when sending (an agent who left can't be messaged).
2. **Conversations belong to their participants**, not to a tenant: `conversations` (one-to-one or a group of up to 20, optional name, where it started), `conversation_participants` (read up to which message, when) and `messages` (text up to 4,000 characters, reply to, edited, deleted for everyone). Only participants can open one; anyone else gets a 404. Row-level security isn't used for these tables because a conversation spans tenants (a team agent and the brokerage's broker); the participant check is in one place (`Conversations::participant`) and on the live channel. Tenant-owned messaging data (announcements, B6.1b) keeps row-level security.
3. **Edits and deletes for 5 minutes.** The sender can edit (marked "Edited") or delete for everyone (the words go; "This message was deleted" stays) within 5 minutes. A deleted account leaves its messages as "Former member".
4. **Live through Laravel Reverb.** Each change is broadcast at once (`ShouldBroadcastNow`) on private channels: `users.{id}` (the inbox: `message.posted`) and `conversations.{uuid}` (`message.posted`, `message.changed`, `conversation.read`), authorised for participants with the flag on. The web signs channels with its session (`/broadcasting/auth`, `@laravel/echo-react`), the phone with its token (`/api/v1/broadcasting/auth`, `laravel-echo` with `pusher-js`), reading the public key and address from `/api/v1/app-config`. A message is saved before it is broadcast, and an unreachable Reverb never fails a send (`Realtime::send`); screens catch up on load. Reverb scales horizontally with Redis (`REVERB_SCALING_ENABLED`). When the app is in the background, push carries messages (B6.2).
5. **Behind the `messages` flag** (ADR 0022): REOS staff first, then tenants on screen 70. Off for a person means a 404 on the pages, the API and the channels, and no menu entry.
6. **Screens.** Web: Messages for every role (provider screen 26): conversation list with unread counts, the open conversation, New message with only the people you may message; the menu shows unread. Phone: Messages from Today (unread count), a conversation, New message.

7. **Attachments (B6.1b).** Up to 5 images or PDFs per message, 20 MB each, stored on the content disk under `messages/<conversation>/` and virus-scanned by the same ClamAV scanner as lesson files (`ScanMessageAttachment`; an unreachable scanner retries; production refuses unscanned files). A file shows "Checking for viruses…" until clean, then opens through a 30-minute signed link; an infected file is deleted and marked removed. Deleting a message for everyone deletes its files.
8. **Report and block (B6.1b).** On every conversation. A block works both ways for one-to-one messages (neither can message the other; neither is offered in New message) and the blocked person no longer sees read state. A report copies the reported person's last 20 messages as they were (so a later edit or delete can't hide them), is audited, and emails the tenant's admins (with the copy) and REOS's super admins; REOS reviews and marks it handled on Compliance and audit (screen 71). `message_reports` keeps row-level security (written in platform scope for the tenant where the conversation started).
9. **Announcements (B6.1b).** Brokerage, school and association admins send to all members or a group (roles, teams, states); recipients are fixed when it's sent (`announcement_recipients`, row-level security on the sending tenant) and read their own across tenants (a team agent gets the brokerage's). Unread announcements show above Messages on the web and on Today on the phone, live (`announcement.posted`), until marked read; the admin sees "Read by 82 of 140".

10. **One notification centre (B6.2a).** Every notification REOS sends extends `CentreNotification`: it names its kind (`NotificationKind`: defaults per channel, urgent, locked, digestible, group) and its inbox line; `NotificationCentre` picks the channels from the person's choices. The inbox (Laravel's database channel, the bell on the web and the phone) is silent and always immediate. Security and account messages (new device, account deletion, privacy requests, receipts, failed payments) can't be turned off.
11. **Quiet hours** (default 9 PM to 7 AM in the person's time zone; on by default): an alert that isn't urgent goes to `held_notifications` (serialized, with the channels it was going to use) and `reos:notifications:release` (every 5 minutes) sends it when quiet hours end, claiming each row first so it goes once. Urgent kinds (new device, account deletion, failed payment, support access, integration paused, feature rolled back, session starting) still arrive.
12. **Digests:** a kind the person chose as daily or weekly is gathered in `digest_items`; `reos:notifications:digests` (hourly) sends one email at 8 AM in their time zone (weekly on Mondays).
13. **What was sent** is logged per kind, channel and outcome (`notification_deliveries`: sent, held, released, digested, failed), never the words.
14. **Settings:** Settings › Notifications on the web (every role) and Me › Notifications on the phone: each kind by channel, digests, quiet hours and the time zone they follow (the browser's or phone's zone offered).

15. **Push through Expo (B6.2b).** A phone sends its Expo push token after the person allows notifications (`POST /api/v1/me/push-devices`); the system prompt only follows a tap on Turn on (Today's card or Me › Notifications), never at launch. Each token belongs to the phone's sign-in (`push_devices.personal_access_token_id`, cascade), so signing out or revoking the phone in Devices removes it; a phone that signs in as someone else moves the token to them. `PushChannel` sends to every phone (title, short body, the badge = unread inbox, the app path a tap opens, Android channel `messages` or `alerts`, high priority and a 1-hour lifetime for urgent kinds). Expo's `DeviceNotRegistered` removes a phone at once (ticket) or 15 minutes later (`reos:push:receipts`, receipts kept a day). Push is a channel like email: on by default for every kind, held in quiet hours, gathered into digests, turned off per kind. Off in production until Expo push is set up in Settings › Integrations (`expo-push`); the simulator keeps an outbox elsewhere.
16. **Messages push when the person isn't looking (B6.2b).** Each message sends `NewMessage` to the other participants (not to anyone who blocked the sender): push only by default (the Messages badge already counts chats, so not the inbox), collapsed per conversation (`collapseId`), titled "Linda Park · 3 new messages" when several wait. Laravel's `shouldSend` drops it if the person read the conversation first or the message was deleted, so a chat held overnight isn't pushed in the morning once read. While REOS is open on that conversation, the phone doesn't show it.
17. **Texts only with consent (B6.2c).** A person adds a US mobile number and verifies it with a code (Twilio Verify; five wrong codes lock it for 15 minutes; a number can belong to one account), then turns on "Get texts from REOS", which shows the words carriers require and records the SMS consent (`consent_records`, web or app) and sends the confirmation text. SMS consent never defaults on: only a granted record counts, checked when choosing channels and again by `SmsChannel` at the moment of sending (a STOP may arrive while a text waits for the morning). Texts are a channel per kind, off by default, never for locked security kinds, and follow quiet hours and digests. Turning texts on from Settings › Privacy goes through the same path. Removing the number withdraws consent; people who sign in with their phone keep it.
18. **STOP, START and HELP (B6.2c).** Twilio posts replies to `/webhooks/twilio-sms`, signed with the auth token (HMAC-SHA1 of the URL and sorted fields; unsigned only on the simulator outside production). STOP and its synonyms withdraw consent for that number (recorded as by SMS), START and its synonyms give it back, and HELP answers with who we are, help contacts, frequency, rates and how to stop. Twilio's Advanced Opt-Out confirms STOP itself.

## Consequences

- Who can message whom is code, reviewed with the journeys; a tenant can't open messaging to everyone.
- Participant-scoped tables need their access check kept in the service and the channel; tests cover both.
- Reverb is another process to run and monitor (B6 platform health); the app degrades to refresh-on-open without it.
- Texts in production wait for Twilio 10DLC brand and campaign approval (register); until then texts stay on the simulator outside production and Settings shows them as not available yet.
- Push needs the EAS project id in the app config and the Apple push key and Firebase project (store accounts) before TestFlight; until then development builds use Expo's sandbox.
