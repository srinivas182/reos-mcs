# Privacy notice

This notice explains what REOS keeps about you, why, and the choices you have. REOS is run by Mayura Consultancy Services. A lawyer reviews this notice before launch; we will ask you again if the wording changes.

## What we keep

Your name, email or phone, language and state; your roles in brokerages, schools or associations; your licenses and CE; your course progress, to-dos, calendar and the tools you connect; purchases (never card details); and a record of what you agreed to. The full list, table by table, is in the data map we keep for regulators.

## Why

To run REOS for you: sign-in, learning, licenses, your work with your broker, school or association, payments, security and support. We never sell your data, and customer data is never used to train AI models.

## AI help

Some features send your own words to Claude on Amazon Bedrock to help you (for example, turning a spoken note into to-dos). It is never stored by the AI service or used for training. You can turn this off in your privacy settings; REOS then uses its built-in helper.

## Text messages

If you agree to texts, we send account and service messages only (codes, reminders, session alerts). Message and data rates may apply; how often depends on your settings. Reply STOP to stop and HELP for help. Your phone number and your agreement to receive texts are never shared with third parties for their marketing.

## Who sees your data

People in your organization see what each screen says they see. Service providers you connect with see your contact details only after you both agree. Our vendors (hosting, email, texts, payments, video) handle data only to run REOS.

## Your choices and rights

Under the Texas Data Privacy and Security Act you can see, correct, download and delete your data, and opt out of uses you don't want. Use Settings › Privacy on the web or Me › Privacy in the app, or write to privacy@reos.com. We answer within 45 days.

## Keeping data

We keep your data while your account is open. When you delete it, it is removed after 30 days, except records the law asks us to keep (such as tax records and what you agreed to), which are kept without your name.
